# Scalable Model-Based Clustering with Sequential Monte Carlo

## Overview

Scalable Model-Based Clustering with Sequential Monte Carlo, or Split SMC is a is a scalable model-based clustering method based on Sequential Monte Carlo (SMC) for grouping large datasets into clusters, especially in online settings where data arrive over time.

## WHAT CAN Split SMC do

Split SMC was developed to make model-based clustering more scalable and practical for very large, online datasets by using Sequential Monte Carlo. It was motivated in part by knowledge base construction, where partial entity observations must be grouped accurately into complete records.

A detailed discussion of Split SMC, including how it was developed and tested, can be found in our paper at: [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=EVDivDL9jD)

## INTENDED USEs

Split SMC is best suited for clustering large, growing datasets where the number of clusters is unknown, especially online or streaming settings with substantial uncertainty over cluster assignments. It is particularly well suited to model-based clustering tasks such as grouping partial entity observations into complete entity records for knowledge base construction.

Split SMC is being shared with the research community to facilitate reproduction of our results and foster further research in this area.

Split SMC is intended to be used by domain experts who are independently capable of evaluating the quality of outputs before acting on them.

## OUT-of-scope uses

Split SMC is not well suited for tasks that require the number of clusters to be fixed in advance, or settings without a suitable probabilistic cluster-generation model.

We do not recommend using Split SMC in commercial or real-world applications without further testing and development. It is being released for research purposes.

Split SMC was not designed or evaluated for all possible downstream purposes. Developers should consider its inherent limitations as they select use cases, and evaluate and mitigate for accuracy, safety, and fairness concerns specific to each intended downstream use.

Without further testing and development, Split SMC should not be used in sensitive domains where inaccurate outputs could suggest actions that lead to injury or negatively impact an individual's legal, financial, or life opportunities.

We do not recommend using Split SMC in the context of high-risk decision making (e.g. in law enforcement, legal, finance, or healthcare).

## How to get started

To begin using Split SMC, clone the repository, install the dependencies with `uv sync --locked`, install the MS-KeBAB dependency if needed, and then run the provided evaluation scripts in the repository to reproduce clustering experiments.

## Evaluation

Split SMC was evaluated on clustering tasks that require grouping individual observations into their underlying latent clusters, including two synthetic 2D problems (overlapping circles and Gaussian-mixture data) and knowledge-base construction tasks built from REBEL and TweetNERD, where fragmented text mentions or entity attributes must be grouped into the correct real-world entities.

A detailed discussion of our evaluation methods and results can be found in our paper at: [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=EVDivDL9jD)

### EVALUATION METHODS

We used precision, recall, F1, log-posterior density, and runtime metrics to measure Split SMC’s performance.

We compared the performance of Split SMC against vanilla Sequential Monte Carlo, greedy clustering, Gibbs sampling / Metropolis-within-Gibbs MCMC, and agglomerative clustering using two synthetic 2D clustering benchmarks and three text clustering benchmarks derived from REBEL and TweetNERD.

### EVALUATION RESULTS

At a high level, we found that Split SMC performed strongly on large-scale online clustering tasks, generally outperforming vanilla sequential methods and often matching or exceeding offline baselines, while scaling to larger problems where some offline methods became impractical. One text benchmark was an exception, where vanilla SMC achieved better accuracy.

## LIMITATIONS

Split SMC was developed for research and experimental purposes. Further testing and validation are needed before considering its application in commercial or real-world scenarios.
